I'm Layla, a content strategist, writer, and video creator passionate about helping brands tell stories that resonate with the right audience.

Over the years, I've published 300+ articles and threads, created 30+ videos, and built an engaged community of 1,500+ followers across platforms through consistency, valuable insights, and authentic storytelling. My work regularly reaches 2,000+ organic views per piece, proving that meaningful content doesn't need to rely on paid promotion to make an impact.

Beyond creating content, I enjoy building communities. I've organized 10 online and in-person events, bringing creators, entrepreneurs, and curious minds together for conversations that extend beyond the timeline.

I believe the best content isn't measured only by impressions — it's measured by the conversations it starts, the trust it builds, and the actions it inspires. Whether it's a long-form article, a video series, a thought-provoking thread, or a content strategy for a growing brand, my goal is always the same: create content that people remember, trust, and share.

Process

How a project actually runs

Five stages, repeated every cycle — the order matters, because each stage feeds the next.

01

Discover

Every project starts with understanding your goals, audience, and brand. Before creating anything, I take the time to learn what success looks like for you.

02

Research

I analyze your audience, industry, competitors, and content opportunities to uncover ideas that have the greatest potential to resonate.

03

Strategy

Using those insights, I develop a clear content direction — defining the topics, formats, messaging, and distribution plan that support your objectives.

04

Create

This is where ideas come to life. Whether it's an article, video, thread, or campaign, every piece is crafted with purpose, clarity, and quality.

05

Publish & Optimize

Content doesn't end when it's published. I monitor performance, gather insights, and refine future content to improve engagement and long-term results.
